# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Sports Intelligence aims to be an open and welcoming community for people interested in exploring sports through culture, business, economics, tourism, technology, society and performance.

We welcome people regardless of background, experience, identity, location or level of technical knowledge.

We are committed to making participation respectful, constructive and accessible.

## Expected behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and consideration.
- Be open to different experiences and interpretations.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Make room for newcomers and people with less specialist knowledge.
- Give appropriate credit to contributors and sources.
- Discuss athletes, teams, fans and communities responsibly.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes when they are identified.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Insults or demeaning comments about someone's identity or background.
- Deliberate intimidation or repeated disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Plagiarism or knowingly misrepresenting another person's work.
- Trolling or sustained bad-faith disruption of discussions.

## Handling concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that conflicts with this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer through the repository.

Please include relevant context or links where possible. Concerns will be reviewed and handled with discretion. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a reminder, removal of content, temporary restrictions or exclusion from the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other spaces where someone is representing Sports Intelligence.

## Attribution

This document is adapted from common open-source community conduct practices and is intended to establish clear expectations for this project.
