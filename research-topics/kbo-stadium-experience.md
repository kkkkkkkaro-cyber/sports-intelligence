## Starting point

A live sporting event can offer much more than watching a competition. A stadium visit may combine sport, entertainment, food, socialising, collective rituals, identity and a sense of belonging.

Korean professional baseball (KBO) offers an interesting context for exploring how these elements come together in the spectator experience.

This topic begins with a question rather than an assumed answer:

**What motivates people to attend KBO games, and how much of the appeal comes from the experience around the game itself?**

## Questions to explore

### The live experience
- What do spectators value most about attending in person?
- How do cheering routines, music, performances and crowd participation shape the experience?
- How do food, venue design and socialising contribute to a stadium visit?

### Fans and culture
- How do shared rituals create a sense of community or identity?
- Do motivations differ between long-time fans, casual spectators and first-time visitors?
- How might age, social context or group size shape the experience?

### Business and entertainment
- How do teams and leagues package the match as a wider entertainment product?
- What roles might sponsorship, merchandise, food and media play in the stadium economy?
- How does the live experience connect with online fan communities and social media?

### Tourism and comparison
- Do people travel to attend games or visit particular stadiums?
- What might visitors from overseas find distinctive or unfamiliar?
- How does the KBO experience compare with NPB, MLB, or other leagues? Comparisons should account for differences in context rather than assume that leagues are directly equivalent.

### Digital culture
- How do short-form videos, fan cams and social media shape expectations of attending?
- Does online content encourage people to visit, or can it satisfy some of the desire to experience the event remotely?
- How are AI-generated or digitally mediated sports experiences changing the way audiences imagine live attendance?

## Possible approaches

- **Desk research:** academic literature, official league materials, industry reports and journalism.
- **Comparative case studies:** stadium experiences across leagues and countries.
- **Digital observation:** publicly available social media content and online fan discussions, respecting platform rules and privacy.
- **Fan perspectives:** voluntary interviews, surveys or informal discussions, with appropriate consent and ethical care.
- **Data exploration:** attendance, ticketing, venue, revenue or tourism data where reliable public sources are available.

## Evidence and source notes

When sharing a claim or statistic, include the source and link, publication or collection date, geographic and time-period context, definitions and known limitations. Avoid treating promotional material or anecdotal posts as representative evidence of all fans.

## How to contribute

Share an experience, suggest a research question, add a useful source, propose a comparison or help organise evidence into a case study. You do not need to code or use GitHub to contribute to the discussion.

## Open questions for the community

- What do you think is the most distinctive part of attending a KBO game?
- Which audience groups should we understand better?
- Which other leagues provide the most useful comparisons, and why?
- What evidence would help us distinguish the appeal of the game from the appeal of the overall event?
